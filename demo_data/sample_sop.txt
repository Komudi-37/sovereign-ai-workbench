STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE
=============================

PUBLIC DEMONSTRATION DATA — NOT CONFIDENTIAL

Document No: SOP-MAINT-001
Title: Rotating Equipment Maintenance Standard
Revision: 3.0
Effective Date: January 1, 2025
Classification: INTERNAL — DEMONSTRATION DATA

1. PURPOSE
----------
This standard defines the requirements for preventive and corrective
maintenance of rotating equipment including pumps, compressors, turbines,
and fans within the demonstration refinery complex.

2. SCOPE
--------
This SOP applies to all rotating equipment with:
- Motor rating > 5 kW
- Operating pressure > 2 bar
- Operating temperature > 50°C
- Critical service designation

3. RESPONSIBILITIES
-------------------
3.1 Maintenance Supervisor: Schedule and oversee maintenance activities
3.2 Maintenance Technician: Execute maintenance tasks per this procedure
3.3 Operations Supervisor: Coordinate equipment isolation and handover
3.4 Inspection Engineer: Conduct condition assessments

4. VIBRATION MONITORING STANDARDS
----------------------------------
4.1 Alert Levels (per ISO 10816):
    - Normal: < 2.8 mm/s RMS
    - Watch:  2.8 – 4.5 mm/s RMS
    - Alert:  4.5 – 7.1 mm/s RMS
    - Danger: > 7.1 mm/s RMS

4.2 When vibration exceeds Alert level:
    a) Notify Maintenance Supervisor within 4 hours
    b) Increase monitoring frequency to daily
    c) Schedule corrective action within 7 days
    d) Document in Maintenance Management System

4.3 When vibration exceeds Danger level:
    a) Notify Maintenance Supervisor immediately
    b) Notify Operations Supervisor for potential shutdown
    c) Initiate emergency maintenance assessment
    d) Equipment must not continue operation if safety risk exists

5. TEMPERATURE MONITORING STANDARDS
------------------------------------
5.1 Bearing Temperature Limits:
    - Normal: < 65°C above ambient
    - Watch:  65–80°C above ambient
    - Alert:  80–95°C above ambient
    - Trip:   > 95°C above ambient

5.2 When bearing temperature exceeds Alert level:
    a) Verify lubricant condition and level
    b) Check cooling system operation
    c) Increase monitoring frequency
    d) Schedule inspection within 48 hours

6. BEARING MAINTENANCE
----------------------
6.1 Routine Inspection Interval:
    - Critical service: Every 3 months
    - Normal service:   Every 6 months
    - Low criticality:  Every 12 months

6.2 Bearing Replacement Criteria:
    a) Vibration consistently above Alert level
    b) Temperature consistently above Watch level
    c) Lubricant analysis shows metallic contamination > 50 ppm
    d) Visual inspection reveals spalling, pitting, or wear
    e) Bearing has exceeded manufacturer's recommended life

6.3 Post-Replacement Verification:
    a) Alignment check (laser preferred)
    b) Vibration baseline measurement
    c) Temperature measurement at 1, 4, and 24 hours
    d) Flow/pressure verification against design values

7. HEAT EXCHANGER MAINTENANCE
------------------------------
7.1 Performance Monitoring:
    - Track approach temperature monthly
    - Track pressure drop monthly
    - Track flow rate weekly

7.2 Fouling Assessment Criteria:
    a) Approach temperature increase > 5°C from clean baseline
    b) Pressure drop increase > 20% from clean baseline
    c) Flow rate decrease > 10% from design flow

7.3 Cleaning Interval:
    - Cooling water service:  Every 6 months (4 months in warm season)
    - Process service:        Per fouling assessment
    - Critical service:       Per condition monitoring

7.4 Cleaning Methods:
    a) Chemical cleaning: Preferred for biological/scale fouling
    b) Mechanical cleaning: For heavy deposits
    c) Hydrojetting: For stubborn deposits

8. DOCUMENTATION REQUIREMENTS
------------------------------
8.1 All maintenance activities must be documented in the CMMS
8.2 Required documentation:
    a) Work order number
    b) Equipment tag
    c) Description of work performed
    d) Parts used
    e) Pre- and post-maintenance measurements
    f) Inspector sign-off

9. ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
----------------------
9.1 Equipment is accepted for return to service when:
    a) All measurements within Normal range
    b) No visible defects or leaks
    c) Safety devices verified functional
    d) Operations notified and agreed to accept
    e) Documentation complete in CMMS
