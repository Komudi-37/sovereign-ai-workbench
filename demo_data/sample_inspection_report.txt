FINDINGS
--------

Finding 1: Centrifugal Pump P-101 — Bearing Degradation
  Severity: Medium
  Status: CORRECTED

  During routine monitoring on January 15-16, 2025, increasing temperature
  and vibration trends were observed on Pump P-101:
  - Temperature increased from 45.2°C to 62.4°C over 32 hours
  - Vibration increased from 2.1 mm/s to 4.5 mm/s
  - Flow rate decreased from 120.5 m³/h to 102.0 m³/h

  Root Cause: Drive-end bearing showed signs of wear and early-stage
  spalling. Lubrication analysis confirmed metallic particle contamination.

  Corrective Action: Bearing replaced on January 17, 2025. Post-maintenance
  readings confirmed normal operation (46.0°C, 2.0 mm/s, 121.5 m³/h).

  Recommendation: Reduce bearing inspection interval from 6 months to
  3 months. Add online vibration monitoring if budget permits.


Finding 2: Heat Exchanger HX-201 — Fouling
  Severity: Low-Medium
  Status: CORRECTED

  On January 18, 2025, elevated shell-side temperature (88.5°C → 91.2°C)
  and reduced flow rate (85.3 → 76.0 m³/h) indicated progressive fouling.

  Root Cause: Biological and scale fouling on tube surfaces, consistent
  with seasonal cooling water quality variations.

  Corrective Action: Chemical cleaning performed on January 18, 2025.
  Post-cleaning readings confirmed restored performance (82.0°C, 85.0 m³/h).

  Recommendation: Schedule cleaning at 4-month intervals during
  warm-weather months. Consider online fouling monitor installation.


Finding 3: Compressor C-301 — Minor Vibration Increase
  Severity: Low
  Status: MONITORING

  Brief vibration increase observed on January 16 (4.5-4.8 mm/s vs
  baseline 3.5 mm/s). Normalized by January 17 without intervention.

  Likely Cause: Temporary process upset (feed composition variation).

  Recommendation: Continue routine monitoring. No maintenance required
  at this time.
